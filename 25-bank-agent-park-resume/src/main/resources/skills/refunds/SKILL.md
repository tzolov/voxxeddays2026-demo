# Refund Handling

## Policy

- Refunds for duplicate or erroneous charges are approved automatically when the
  duplicate transaction is confirmed in the customer's transaction history.
- Approved refunds are credited back to the customer's account within 3-5 business days.
- Refunds above CHF 500 require a manual review by the fraud team and may take up to
  10 business days.
- A refund request never requires blocking the customer's card.

## What to tell the customer

- Quote the amount of the charge and the settlement window that applies to it.
- For an amount above CHF 500, say that the fraud team reviews the refund first.
- Do not promise a date: give the window in business days.
- You cannot submit or track a refund yourself, so do not offer to. The customer reports the
  duplicate charge in the banking app, under Cards > Dispute a charge.
